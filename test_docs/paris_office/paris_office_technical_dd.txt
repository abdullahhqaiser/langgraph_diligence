2. STRUCTURAL ASSESSMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 GENERAL
    The building is of reinforced concrete (RC) frame construction with a
    post-tensioned flat-slab structural system on each floor. The core walls
    are RC shear walls providing lateral stability. Foundation type:
    deep piled (60 No. bored piles to 18m depth; Franki EDP piles).

2.2 FINDINGS
    External façade: Full-height curtain walling system (Schüco AWS 90.SI+).
    Units inspected on Floors 1–3 and 8 (highest accessible without MEWP).
    No delamination, cracking or joint failures observed. ✓

    Ground-floor arcade: Some minor cracking to render at column base
    (arcade col. C7 & C9). Consistent with normal settlement in newly
    completed structures. Monitoring recommended over 12 months.
    REMEDIAL: Raking-out and refilling with flexible sealant; cost EUR 3,500.

    Roof structure: Green sedum roof on podium (Level 1 setback) inspected.
    Waterproofing membrane (Sarnafil TS 77-18 E) appeared intact with no
    visible punctures. Drainage outlets clear. ✓
    Solar PV array (128 kWp, 320 panels): Panel condition satisfactory.
    No visible physical damage. Inverter room accessible; inverters (Fronius
    Symo 25.0-3-M × 4) appear serviceable. CAUTION: Full PV commissioning
    report not yet provided in data room (see Section 6).

2.3 STRUCTURAL OPINION
    No structural defects noted. No signs of differential settlement.
    The structure appears to be in good condition commensurate with its age.

3. BUILDING SERVICES (MEP)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 HVAC
    System type: 4-pipe fan coil units (FCU) throughout office floors.
    Adiabatic free cooling plant on roof. Primary plant: 2 × Climaveneta
    ERACS-A 1600V air-cooled chillers (2 × 1,600 kW cooling capacity).
    Heating: 2 × Buderus Logamax Plus GB172 condensing boilers.

    FCUs inspected on Floors 1, 5 and 7 (occupied floors). All FCUs found
    commissioned and serviceable. Filter media recently changed (confirmed by
    maintenance log). ✓

    Commissioning test report (Siemens, Oct 2025): ONLY 74% of FCUs shown
    in final commissioning data. 127 of 170 units signed off; remaining 43
    are on Floors 3 & 4 (vacant). Vendor has stated units were commissioned
    but data not uploaded.
    ⚠ FLAG A — Request full Siemens commissioning report for Floors 3 & 4
      before or at practical completion of any FM handover. Risk: low
      (units are new and same spec), but completeness required.

3.2 ELECTRICAL
    Main LV switchboard (ASTER by Legrand, 2,500A main incomer): visually
    examined. Labelling complete. Cable containment tidy. ✓
    UPS system: 2N configuration (2 × 300 kVA Eaton 93PS). Fully loaded
    only to 12% of rated capacity (dedicated to IT room on each floor and
    emergency services). Adequate headroom for tenants' IT loads. ✓
    Generator: 1 × Caterpillar C9 diesel genset (250 kVA) in basement.
    Last test run: January 22, 2026 (logged in BMS). ✓
    EV Charging: 34 dedicated charging points (WALLBOX Copper SB; 22 kW).
    Installed on B1 level only. 78 remaining parking spaces have cabling
    "ready for fit-out" but chargers not yet installed. Regulatory minimum met.

3.3 PLUMBING & DRAINAGE
    Domestic water services: copper throughout; lagged in accessible plant
    areas. Booster pump set (Grundfos HYDRO MPC-E) serviceable. ✓
    Sanitary drainage: gravity to public sewer. No backflow prevention
    issues noted. Building inspection chamber in B2 accessible.
    Legionella risk: Schematic reviewed; no evident dead-legs in distribution.
    L8 risk assessment required by first tenant occupation (landlord duty).
    ⚠ FLAG B — L8 assessment and water-temperature monitoring regime
      must be put in place before Floors 3 & 4 are let. Estimated cost: EUR 4,200.

3.4 FIRE PROTECTION
    Sprinkler system: Wet pipe throughout (Minimax; EN 12845 compliant).
    SDIS inspection completed December 2025. ✓
    Fire alarm: Hochiki FX system; L1 coverage. All sounders and detectors
    verified on inspection. ✓
    Emergency lighting: Self-contained 3-hour maintained units. ✓
    Smoke ventilation: Natural smoke ventilation via automated AOVs in
    stairwells. All actuators tested January 2026. ✓

3.5 ELEVATORS
    4 × KONE MonoSpace 700 passenger lifts (1,000 kg / 13-person) + 1 × KONE
    cargo lift (2,500 kg). TÜV Rheinland certificates obtained for all 5 lifts
    (January 2026). First statutory inspection due January 2028. ✓
