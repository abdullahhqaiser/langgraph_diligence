1. INVESTMENT THESIS SUMMARY

creation plan to grow revenue from £68M to £115M+ and expand EBITDA

2. VALUE CREATION LEVERS (1-100 DAY PLAN)
──────────────────────────────────────────

2.1 Pricing Power Analysis
Current pricing:
  • Average hourly machine rate: £85/hr (below market median of £95)
  • PPE has NOT raised machining rates in 3 years (founder conservatism)
  • Contract re-pricing opportunity: 12 contracts up for renewal in 2026

Lever:
  → Implement 5-8% price increase on FY2026 renewals
  → Expected EBITDA impact: +£1.2-1.8M
  → Low risk: PPE is sole-source on 60% of parts (switching cost high)
  
  ⚠ Risk: Rolls-Royce LTA includes annual cost-down clause (3%). Net
    price increase on Rolls-Royce work = 0-2%. Non-Rolls contracts
    offer more pricing flexibility (8-12% increase achievable).

4. BOLT-ON M&A STRATEGY
────────────────────────

4.1 Buy-and-Build Thesis
PPE is ideally positioned as a platform for aerospace/medical precision
machining consolidation in the UK Midlands/North. Fragmented market
with 200+ sub-£20M precision machining companies.

Criteria:
  Revenue: £5-20M
  EBITDA margin: >15%
  Certifications: AS9100 or ISO 13485
  Geography: UK Midlands/North (within 50 miles of Sheffield)
  EV/EBITDA: 5-7x (small company discount)

    Revenue: £12M | EBITDA: £2.4M (20%) | EV: £14M (5.8x)
    Capability: 5-axis CNC, aerospace qualification
    Synergies: £0.5M (procurement, shared overhead)
    Location: Rotherham (8 miles from Sheffield)
    Risk: Low (clean company, sole owner retirement)

4.3 M&A Value Creation Model
  Combined pre-synergy EBITDA: £14.8M + £6.7M = £21.5M
  Synergies (Year 2 run-rate): +£1.2M
