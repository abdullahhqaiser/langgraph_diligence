5. MANAGEMENT'S OWN RISK ASSESSMENT
─────────────────────────────────────
(From management presentation, December 2025)

"We see the following risks to the MBO plan:
  1. Aerospace downturn could reduce revenue 10-15% (cyclical risk)
  2. Skilled labor shortages remain acute — we need 20 additional
     CNC machinists over the next 2 years, and the Sheffield pool
     is very competitive
  3. Titanium and Inconel pricing has risen 25% YoY — we have
     limited ability to pass through to OEMs under fixed contracts
  4. Our IT infrastructure is aging — the Sage 200 ERP needs
     significant investment to support Industry 4.0 goals
  5. We have never done an acquisition — this is new territory
     for us and we will need sponsor support and potentially an
     experienced M&A integration hire"

Management self-assessment: Honest, no sandbagging. Team is aware
of limitations and has asked for sponsor operational support.
