B.1 Rolls-Royce Long-Term Agreement (LTA) — Full Summary

  Agreement date:      15 March 2024
  Term:                5 years (to 14 March 2029)
  Renewal:             Auto-renew for 2-year periods unless 12-month notice
  
  Scope:
    Part families:     Turbine disc forgings (machining), fan case components,
                       LP shaft components, bearing housings
    Programs:          Trent XWB, Trent 7000, Trent 1000 (aftermarket)
    Annual value:      £13-16M (volume-dependent, firm orders + options)
  
  Pricing:
    Base rates:        Fixed for Year 1; annual escalation = CPI - 3%
    Material pass-through: Titanium and Inconel at actual cost + 8% handling
    Cost-down:         3% annual productivity saving required from Year 2
    Raw material risk: Shared (PPE bears first 5% variance, Rolls absorbs excess)
  
  Quality:
    Defect rate threshold:  <500 PPM (parts per million)
    Delivery OTIF target:   98%
    Current performance:    PPM = 180; OTIF = 97.5%
  
  IP:
    All designs remain Rolls-Royce IP
    PPE may not manufacture for other engine OEMs using Rolls designs
    Process IP and tooling design: PPE retains ownership
  
  Termination:
    Rolls-Royce: 6 months notice for convenience; immediate for quality failure
    PPE: 6 months notice + 12 months wind-down obligation
    Change of control: NO termination right (favorable for MBO)
  
  ⚠ Key clause: Annual 3% cost-down is aggressive given input cost inflation.
    In practice, PPE has achieved 1.5-2% cost-down through efficiency,
    absorbing the gap from margin. Long-term margin compression risk.

B.2 Smith+Nephew Master Supply Agreement — Key Terms

  Change of control:   S+N may terminate with 90 days written notice
  Product liability:   PPE indemnifies up to £5M per incident
  Insurance required:  £10M product liability, £5M public liability
  Quality:             ISO 13485 maintenance is a condition; loss of
                       certification = automatic termination
  Exclusivity:         None (PPE may supply competitors)
  Minimum volumes:     None (purchase order basis)
  
  ⚠ Change of control clause is the primary commercial risk of the MBO.
    S+N revenue = £7.5M (11% of total). Recommend: Pre-signing letter
    from S+N procurement confirming continuation of relationship post-MBO.
    Alternative: Negotiate waiver or consent in SPA as condition precedent.
