5. MARKET GROWTH DRIVERS & HEADWINDS
──────────────────────────────────────
Growth Drivers:
  ✓ Narrowbody aircraft order backlog: ~9,200 aircraft (Airbus + Boeing)
    → 8-10 years of production visibility
  ✓ UK defense spending increase: 2.5% GDP target by 2030
  ✓ Nearshoring: Medical device companies moving machining from China/India
    back to UK/Europe (quality control + supply chain resilience)
  ✓ Engine MRO demand: Aging widebody fleet drives aftermarket parts
  ✓ Industry 4.0: Digital machining creates productivity improvements

Headwinds:
  ⚠ Titanium & Inconel price volatility (+25% YoY, Jan 2026)
  ⚠ Aerospace OEM pricing pressure: Airbus demanding 3-5% annual cost
    reductions from supply chain
  ⚠ Skilled labor shortage: CNC machinists scarce in Sheffield region
    (unemployment 3.2%, technical positions 4-6 months to fill)
  ⚠ Brexit friction: UK suppliers face delays at EU border for
    time-sensitive JIT deliveries to Airbus (Toulouse, Hamburg)
  ⚠ Energy costs: UK industrial electricity 40% above EU average
