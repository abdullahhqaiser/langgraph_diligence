1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
────────────────────
Pinnacle Precision Engineering ("PPE" or "the Company") is a leading
UK-based manufacturer of precision-machined components for the aerospace
and defense (A&D) and medical device sectors. The current owner, the
founding family (the Hargreaves), is seeking a management buyout with
PE sponsor backing to fund the next phase of growth.

  Legal Name:            Pinnacle Precision Engineering Ltd.
  Incorporated:          England & Wales (1987)
  Headquarters:          Sheffield, UK
  Employees:             412 FTE (340 production, 72 office/management)
  Revenue (FY2025A):     £68.2M
  Adj. EBITDA (FY2025A): £14.8M (21.7% margin)
  Asking Price:          £105M enterprise value (7.1x LTM EBITDA)
  Transaction Type:      Management Buyout (MBO) with PE sponsor

2. BUSINESS DESCRIPTION
───────────────────────
PPE manufactures high-precision CNC-machined components, assemblies,
and sub-systems for tier-1 aerospace OEMs and medical device companies.
The company operates from two facilities in Sheffield, UK:

  Facility 1 (Attercliffe): 65,000 sq ft — Aerospace production
  Facility 2 (Meadowhall):  28,000 sq ft — Medical device production

Product mix:
  • Aerospace structural components:     42% of revenue
  • Aero engine parts (Rolls-Royce):     23% of revenue
  • Medical device components:            22% of revenue
  • Defense electronics housings:         8% of revenue
  • Other precision machining:            5% of revenue

Key certifications:
  ✓ AS9100 Rev D (aerospace quality management)
  ✓ ISO 13485 (medical device quality management)
  ✓ NADCAP (special processes — heat treatment, NDT)
  ✓ SC21 Gold Award (supply chain excellence)
  ✓ UK MOD approved supplier

─────────────────────────────────────

  Margaret Hargreaves (spouse):          20%

    Robert's health is reportedly good but planning horizon is 12-24 months.
