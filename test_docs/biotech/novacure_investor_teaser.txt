2. UNMET MEDICAL NEED
─────────────────────
NovaCure targets tumors driven by transcriptional addiction — cancers
that rely on super-enhancer mediated gene expression for survival. These
tumors are poorly served by existing CDK4/6 inhibitors (palbociclib,
ribociclib) and checkpoint immunotherapy.

Key statistics:
  • NSCLC patients failing 2L+ therapy: ~185,000 annually (US + EU5)
  • Triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC): ~46,000 new cases/yr (US)
  • 5-year survival for metastatic TNBC: <12%
  • CDK12-loss prevalence in aggressive solid tumors: 8-15%

NVC-4019 targets a distinct mechanism (CDK12/13) not addressed by any
approved or late-stage therapy, creating genuine first-in-class potential.

4. LEAD ASSET: NVC-4019
────────────────────────
Mechanism:      Selective dual CDK12/CDK13 kinase inhibitor
Administration: Oral, once-daily, 200mg tablet
Indication 1:  2L+ NSCLC with CDK12-loss biomarker
Indication 2:  2L+ TNBC (biomarker-selected)
Indication 3:  Prostate cancer (exploratory, preclinical)

Phase 1 Data Summary (ASCO 2025 poster):
  • Dose-limiting toxicity: None observed at therapeutic dose (200mg)
  • MTD not reached; RP2D established at 200mg QD
  • ORR (NSCLC biomarker+): 38% (16/42), DCR: 71% (30/42)
  • Median PFS (NSCLC): 7.2 months (vs. 3.1 months historical 2L docetaxel)
  • Grade 3+ AEs: 12% (neutropenia 7%, thrombocytopenia 5%)
  • No cardiac signals, no dose-dependent hepatotoxicity
