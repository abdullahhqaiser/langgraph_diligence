2. PHASE 1 FIRST-IN-HUMAN STUDY (NVC-4019-001)
───────────────────────────────────────────────
Study Design:
  • 3+3 dose escalation followed by expansion cohorts
  • Dose levels: 25mg, 50mg, 100mg, 150mg, 200mg, 300mg QD
  • N = 78 evaluable patients (dose escalation + expansion)
  • All-comer solid tumors (dose escalation); biomarker-selected
    NSCLC + TNBC (expansion)

Key Results (data cut: October 2025):
  Dose (QD)   N     ORR      DCR      Median PFS   Grade 3+ AE
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  25mg        6     0%       33%      1.8 mo       0%
  50mg        6     0%       50%      2.4 mo       0%
  100mg       8     13%      63%      3.8 mo       0%
  150mg       12    17%      67%      5.1 mo       8%
  200mg       24    33%      71%      7.0 mo       12%
  300mg       6     33%      67%      6.8 mo       33%

  → RP2D selected: 200mg QD (best efficacy/safety balance)
  → MTD: 300mg (DLTs: G3 neutropenia 2/6, G3 thrombocytopenia 1/6)

Biomarker-Selected NSCLC Expansion (200mg, n=42):
  • ORR: 38% (16/42) — all partial responses
  • DCR: 71% (30/42)
  • Median PFS: 7.2 months (95% CI: 5.1 – 9.8)
  • Median DOR: 8.4 months (ongoing in 9/16 responders)

Biomarker-Selected TNBC Expansion (200mg, n=18):
  • ORR: 28% (5/18)
  • DCR: 61% (11/18)
  • Median PFS: 5.8 months (95% CI: 3.2 – 8.1)

3. PHASE 2a REGISTRATIONAL STUDY (NVC-4019-002)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Study Title: "CLARITY-1: A Phase 2a, Open-Label, Multi-Center Study of
NVC-4019 in CDK12-Low Advanced Solid Tumors"

Design:
  • Two-arm, biomarker-selected, single-agent
  • Arm A: NSCLC 2L+ (post platinum + checkpoint inhibitor)
  • Arm B: TNBC 2L+ (post taxane + checkpoint inhibitor)
  • Primary endpoint: ORR by BICR (RECIST 1.1)
  • Secondary: PFS, OS, DOR, safety, PROs, biomarker correlation
  • Target enrollment: 120 patients (80 NSCLC, 40 TNBC)
  • Sites: 35 centers across US, EU, Australia

  ⚠ Enrollment challenge: CDK12-low biomarker prevalence is ~12-18%
    of screened patients. Pre-screening program in place with 15 labs.

Timeline:
  First patient in:        March 2026
  Interim analysis:        Q3 2026 (30% events, NSCLC arm)
  Full enrollment:         Q4 2026
  Primary analysis:        Q2 2027
  FDA EOP2 meeting:        Q3 2027

Success Criteria:
  • NSCLC: ORR ≥25% (historical 2L docetaxel ORR: 10-15%)
  • TNBC: ORR ≥20% (historical sacituzumab: 22%, but CDK12-low enriched)
  • To support Accelerated Approval: ORR + DOR sufficient under Fast Track

4. PLANNED COMBINATION STUDY (NVC-4019-003)
────────────────────────────────────────────
Study: "NVC-4019 + Pembrolizumab in CDK12-Low NSCLC"

Rationale:
  CDK12 inhibition induces tandem duplications → neoantigens
  → enhanced anti-tumor immunity synergizing with anti-PD-1.

Design:
  • Phase 1b dose escalation + expansion
  • NVC-4019 100mg/150mg/200mg QD + pembro 200mg Q3W
  • Target: 45 patients
  • Primary: Safety, RP2D of combination
  • Secondary: ORR, PFS

Timeline:
  IND amendment:           Q4 2026
  First patient in:        Q1 2027
  Dose escalation:         Q1-Q3 2027
  Expansion:               Q4 2027 onwards

5. CLINICAL TRIAL DESIGN ASSESSMENT
────────────────────────────────────
5.1 Design Strengths
  ✓ Biomarker-selected population (higher signal-to-noise)
  ✓ Fast Track designation enables rolling submission
  ✓ Open-label single-arm acceptable for accelerated approval
  ✓ BICR for primary endpoint (regulatory quality)
  ✓ Multi-center (35 sites) reduces single-site bias

5.2 Design Risks
  ⚠ Single-arm design: No randomized comparator; confirmatory
    trial (Ph 3) needed for full approval
  ⚠ Biomarker prevalence: 12-18% screen-positive rate may cause
    enrollment delays (screen-to-enroll ratio ~6:1)
  ⚠ CDx readiness: Companion diagnostic must be validated
    concurrently; any CDx delay → regulatory delay
  ⚠ Small TNBC arm (n=40): May be underpowered for subgroup
    analyses; FDA may require larger confirmatory cohort

5.3 Trial Design vs. Norms
  Parameter            NVC-4019-002      Typical Oncology Ph 2
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Sample size          120               80-200 (adequate)
  Primary endpoint     ORR (BICR)        Standard
  Enrollment timeline  ~8 months         12-18 months (faster due
                                         to biomarker pre-screen)
  # of sites           35                20-40 (adequate)
  CDx requirement      Yes               Increasingly common
