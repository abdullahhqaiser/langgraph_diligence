  Status:        US pending; EU pending
  Claims:        Method of selecting patients using CDK12 IHC + genomic

  Risk:          MODERATE-HIGH — diagnostic method claims face Alice/Mayo
                 challenges in US (patent-eligible subject matter)

  ⚠ Key concern: P4 claims may be vulnerable to 35 USC §101 challenge.

    CDK12 testing approaches.

  Risk:          HIGH — combination claims face obviousness challenge;
                 pembrolizumab is widely combined with oncology agents
  Prosecution:   Response preparing citing unexpected synergy data

1.6 Patent Family P6 — Polymorph
  Status:        US provisional filed; PCT pending
  Claims:        Crystalline Form A of NVC-4019 free base; XRPD pattern
  Risk:          LOW — polymorph patents extend lifecycle but secondary

4. REGULATORY STATUS & PATHWAY
──────────────────────────────
4.1 Current Designations
  ✓ FDA Fast Track Designation — granted December 2024
    (NVC-4019 for CDK12-low 2L+ NSCLC)
  ✓ FDA Orphan Drug Designation — application pending
    (TNBC subtype may qualify for rare disease)
  ⚠ Breakthrough Therapy Designation — not yet applied
    → Will submit BTD request with Phase 2a interim data (Q3 2026)

4.2 US Regulatory Pathway
  Most likely path: Accelerated Approval based on ORR
  • ORR is established surrogate endpoint for oncology AA
  • Fast Track enables rolling submission
  • Confirmatory trial (Phase 3) required post-approval
  • Priority Review likely (8-month review vs. standard 12)

  Timeline estimate:
    Phase 2a data lock:     Q2 2027
    Pre-NDA meeting:        Q3 2027
    Rolling NDA start:      Q4 2027
    NDA complete:           Q1 2028
    PDUFA date:             Q3 2028

4.3 EU Regulatory Pathway
  • Conditional Marketing Authorization (CMA) or standard MAA
  • EMA PRIME eligibility: will apply with Phase 2a data
  • Rapporteur selection: UK MHRA for initial scientific advice
  • Filing: 6-12 months post-FDA submission

4.4 Companion Diagnostic (CDx) Regulatory
  • CDx: Roche/Ventana CDK12 IHC panel + NGS panel
  • CDx PMA pathway: co-submission with NVC-4019 NDA
  • CDx development timeline aligned with Phase 2a
  • CDK12 IHC cutoff validation: ongoing in CLARITY-1 study
  
  ⚠ CDx risk: If CDx validation delayed, FDA may approve drug
    with laboratory-developed test (LDT) provision, but this limits
    commercial reach. Roche CDx partnership mitigates this risk.
